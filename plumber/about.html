Our Mission
Honest Plumbing for Real Families

Founded in 2014 by James Davidson, FlowFix Plumbing started with one truck, two technicians, and a simple belief: plumbing should be done right, explained clearly, and priced fairly. A decade later, we've completed over 2,400 jobs across Demo City and the surrounding areas.

We're not a franchise or a call center — we're your neighbors. We understand the homes, the pipes, and the people of Demo City in a way that out-of-town companies simply can't.
